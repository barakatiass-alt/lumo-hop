Lumo Hop

One firefly. A sky full of lanterns. One tap.

A firefly circles a glowing paper lantern. Tap to let it go, and catch the next light before its glow fades. Quick hops build a combo, lanterns burn down if you wait, and the sky changes color as you climb.
