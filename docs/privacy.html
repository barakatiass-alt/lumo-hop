Privacy policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

We collect nothing

Lumo Hop does not collect, store, sell or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

What stays on your device

  • Your best score, the firefly color you picked and the sound setting are saved only on your own device, so the game remembers them.
  • This information never leaves your device and is deleted when you delete the app.

Sharing your score

If you tap the challenge button, the game opens your device's own share sheet with a short message. You choose whether and where to send it. The game does not see or keep it.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages and collects no information from anyone, including children.

Contact

Questions about privacy: open a request on the support page.
